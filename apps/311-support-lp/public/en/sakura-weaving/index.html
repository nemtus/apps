Raising "Sakura Weaving"

Use Web APP "Sakura Weaving" and check on it, your donation amount will be reflected as Sakura petals, and your heartfelt message will be displayed at the same time. If the timing is right, you can see each other's messages.
※When 1XEM to 3XEM are gathered in total, a petal of Sakura appeared.
When 1,000 XEM is accumulated, Sakura petals start to wander about in the air as if they are dancing, after that, one Sakura block will be generated. (One Sakura block = 1,000XEM)
Again the Sakura will return to its original state, and will approach full bloom with each donation. Then another block is chained one after another.
Thankfully we’ve got lots of XEM donations. And at 17th Apr. 2021, Sakura Blocks are almost sticking out from the frame. So we changed the structure of Sakura Blocks into 2-stage configuration.

Step1： Every time 1,000 XEM is accumulated, Sakura will be full bloom. Then turn into 1 gray framed Sakura block.
Step2： 5 gray framed Sakura blocks (=5,000XEM) will be 1 pink framed Sakura block.

1 gray framed block = 1,000 XEM
1 pink framed block = 5,000 XEM
